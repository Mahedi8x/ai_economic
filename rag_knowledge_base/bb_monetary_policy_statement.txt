BANGLADESH BANK MONETARY POLICY STATEMENT (MPS 2025-2026)
CENTRAL BANK OF BANGLADESH, HEAD OFFICE, DHAKA.

1. Monetary Stance & Policy Interest Rates:
To anchor headline and food inflation expectations, Bangladesh Bank maintains a contractionary monetary policy stance. The policy repo rate is maintained at 10.00 percent, with the Standing Lending Facility (SLF) rate at 11.50 percent and the Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate at 8.50 percent. This tightening measures aim to compress aggregate domestic demand and curb imported price pressures.

2. Exchange Rate Management (Crawling Peg System):
The crawling peg arrangement for USD/BDT transactions remains operational with a mid-rate corridor. Authorized Dealer (AD) banks must execute foreign exchange customer transactions within the permissible band around the benchmark. Central bank market vigilance prioritizes interbank foreign exchange market liquidity and curbing unauthorized curb/hundi spreads.

3. Import Discipline and LC Rationalization:
Commercial banks are instructed to maintain 100 percent cash margin requirements for import Letters of Credit (LCs) on luxury and non-essential consumer goods. Essential commodities (Rice, Wheat, Edible Oils, Lentils, Onion, Sugar, and Fertilizers) are strictly exempt from margin requirements, ensuring seamless supply chain flow and price stabilization.

4. Reserve Adequacy (IMF BPM6 Framework):
Gross Foreign Exchange Reserves are accounted strictly in accordance with the IMF BPM6 benchmark. The central bank prioritizes reserve accumulation through enhanced official remittance incentives and rationalizing import payments to preserve minimum import coverage above 3.5 to 4.0 months.
