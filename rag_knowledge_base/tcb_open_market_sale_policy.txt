TRADING CORPORATION OF BANGLADESH (TCB)
MINISTRY OF COMMERCE, GOVERNMENT OF THE PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF BANGLADESH
TCB BHABAN, KAWRAN BAZAR, DHAKA.

1. Open Market Sale (OMS) & Family Card Subsidized Allocation:
To mitigate acute food inflation shocks on low-income and vulnerable populations, TCB operates nationwide truck sale and Family Card distribution operations. The core subsidized basket comprises:
- Fine and Coarse Rice: Allocated at ৳ 30.00 per kg.
- Lentils (Masur Dal): Allocated at ৳ 60.00 per kg.
- Fortified Soybean Oil: Allocated at ৳ 100.00 per liter.
- Granulated Sugar: Allocated at ৳ 70.00 per kg.

2. Strategic Buffer Stock Thresholds:
TCB maintains mandatory emergency national buffer stocks of 2.0 to 2.5 months of aggregate national consumption for refined edible oil, pulses, and onion. If 30-day forecasted food inflation crosses the critical 10.00 percent threshold, automated central market intervention triggers subsidized market offloading to cool retail price spikes.
