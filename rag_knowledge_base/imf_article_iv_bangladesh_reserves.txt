INTERNATIONAL MONETARY FUND (IMF)
ARTICLE IV CONSULTATION & EXTENDED CREDIT FACILITY (ECF/EFF/RSF)
MACROECONOMIC ASSESSMENT REPORT FOR BANGLADESH.

1. Net International Reserves (NIR) Performance Targets:
Under the IMF structural economic reform agenda, Bangladesh Bank commits to rebuilding gross foreign exchange reserves using the standard BPM6 methodology. The floor for Net International Reserves (NIR) is maintained at minimum thresholds to preserve import cover against adverse external shocks.

2. Monetary Tightening and External Solvency:
The IMF mission emphasizes maintaining interest rate flexibility and a tight monetary stance until headline inflation decisively recedes below the 7.00 percent target corridor. To avoid severe currency depreciation shocks, Bangladesh is advised to allow market-driven price discovery in the foreign exchange interbank corridor and phase out non-tariff import restrictions.
